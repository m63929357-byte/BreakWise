HOW IT WORKS

Simple steps. Smarter operations.

BreakWise simplifies employee attendance and break monitoring through a connected and organized workflow.

01

Scan RFID

Employees scan their RFID card to record their attendance when entering or leaving the warehouse.

02

Attendance Recorded

BreakWise automatically records employee time-in and time-out data for centralized monitoring.

03

Monitor Breaks

Scheduled employee breaks are tracked in real time to help administrators monitor current break activities.

04

Alerts & Reports

Administrators can identify overdue breaks and review attendance and break monitoring reports.

ABOUT BREAKWISE

Built for fast-paced warehouse operations.

BreakWise is designed to support warehouse environments where employee attendance and break schedules need to be monitored efficiently.
